I'm a dedicated and detail-focused Revit Modeler with a track record of building accurate BIM models for architectural, structural, and MEP engineering projects — collaborating closely with multidisciplinary teams to keep every model coordinated and clash-free.

From raw PDFs, sketches and point cloud scans to fully detailed, construction-ready Revit models — my process is built around precision, clean documentation, and dependable turnaround for architecture, structural and MEP (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) scopes.
